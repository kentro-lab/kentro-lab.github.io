Common questions

How do I delete my account and my data? In the app, open Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account. This removes your account and is intended to delete your projects and content. See our Privacy Policy for details on retention and deletion.

How is my data handled? Everything is covered in our Privacy Policy — what we collect, how AI features process your content, and your choices.

I found a bug, or I have feedback. Email support@kentrolabs.ai with what happened and, if you can, a screenshot. Details help us fix things faster.
