Privacy Policy

Kentro AI Labs Inc.

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Kentro AI Labs Inc. (“Kentro,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) develops and operates Kentro, a project-management application for construction professionals that includes a built-in AI assistant called Agent K. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Kentro application and related services (collectively, the “Service”), how we use and share it, and the choices and rights available to you.

By creating an account or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Service.

Throughout this policy, “personal information” or “personal data” means information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to you. Where we describe categories of information, purposes, and recipients, we do so in a way intended to cover our current practices and reasonably foreseeable activities of the same kind and for the same purposes.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information.

Account and identity information. When you create an account, we collect information associated with the sign-in method you choose. You may sign in using email and password, or using your Google or Apple account. Depending on the method, we receive and store your display name, email address, an associated profile or avatar image reference, a unique account identifier, and account timestamps (such as when your account was created and when it was last active). We use email verification to confirm control of an email address.

Content you create and upload. The Service is built around the project content you provide. This includes, by category:

  • Projects and project records — projects and their associated records, including tasks, schedules, decisions, budget and cost entries, requests for information, and similar structured project data.
  • Photos and images — photographs and images you capture or upload, including their file contents.
  • Documents — documents you upload (such as PDF and word-processing files), including their file contents and text.
  • Voice recordings and audio — audio you record within the Service, including microphone input captured during voice interactions with Agent K. During voice mode, your microphone audio is streamed in real time to a third-party AI service provider for transcription and response (see Sections 3 and 4).
  • Notes and annotations — notes, annotations, titles, severity or status indicators, and the locations to which they are attached (for example, a point on an image, a page in a document, or a timestamp in an audio recording).
  • Tags and metadata — tags, labels, file names, file types, file sizes, and other descriptive metadata associated with your content.
  • Messages — the messages, prompts, and attachments you exchange with Agent K, along with associated conversation and session records.

Location information from media. We may collect geographic location information associated with your content — for example, precise location coordinates (latitude and longitude) embedded in a photo’s metadata, or location points you or the Service attach to notes and other records. When you add a photo, we may extract and store the precise location coordinates embedded in that photo’s metadata, where present, even though the app does not separately track your device’s location. This location information is associated with the related content. Extraction is best-effort and depends on whether your file and device include such metadata.

AI-derived information. The Service generates information about your content using AI features — for example, extracted text, summaries, suggested tags, identified fields, and similar derived outputs. This derived information is stored alongside the content it describes. See Section 3.

Usage and device information. When you use the Service, we and our providers automatically collect limited technical and usage information, such as device and operating-system characteristics, application version and release information, and similar technical signals. Our analytics collection is limited to the analytics SDK’s automatic events and the names of screens you view; we do not set custom analytics events, user identifiers, or user properties, and analytics is collected only in production builds. We use this analytics and screen-view data to understand and improve how the Service is used, and it generally does not, by itself, identify you. Our diagnostic and crash records are handled separately and are linked to your account identifier, as described below and in Section 5.

Diagnostic and crash information. To keep the Service reliable, we collect diagnostic, error, and crash information. This may include error messages, stack traces, the unique account identifier of the affected user, device and application state, and related technical context. These diagnostic and crash records are linked to your unique account identifier. As described in Section 5, our diagnostic and crash reporting may also capture portions of in-app content — including portions of your spoken (voice) input, the assistant’s spoken responses, and the parameters of voice commands — whenever you use voice features.

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the Service — to create and maintain your account, store and organize your projects and content, and make your data available to you across sessions and devices.
  • To deliver AI features — to operate Agent K and the Service’s AI-assisted features, including analyzing photos and documents, transcribing and responding to voice input, extracting text and fields, suggesting tags, summarizing content, and answering your questions. See Section 3.
  • To authenticate and secure access — to verify your identity, protect accounts, detect and prevent fraud or abuse, and maintain the security and integrity of the Service.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service — to monitor performance, diagnose and fix errors and crashes, understand usage patterns, and develop new and improved features.
  • To communicate with you — to send you service-related messages, respond to your requests, and provide support.
  • To comply with law and protect rights — to comply with applicable legal obligations, enforce our terms, and protect the rights, safety, and property of you, us, and others.

We may process information as needed to perform our agreement with you, in reliance on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Service, with your consent where required, and as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

3. Agent K and AI Processing

Agent K and other AI-assisted features in the Service are powered in part by third-party AI service providers that operate cloud-based AI models. These providers process the content you submit under their own terms and data-governance practices in addition to our agreements with them.

To generate the results you request, content you submit is transmitted to these AI service providers for processing. Depending on the feature you use, the content sent for processing may include the text of your messages and prompts, your conversation and session history, your display name, the contents of photos and documents you select (including images and extracted text), related project and content information surfaced to answer your request (which may include descriptive metadata such as location coordinates extracted from your media and information extracted from your documents, such as dates, amounts, and payment details), and — during voice interactions — your live microphone audio. Voice input is streamed in real time to the AI service provider, where it is transcribed and used to generate spoken and text responses.

Some features may process input locally on your device; where that is the case, that input is not transmitted to our AI service providers for that step.

This content is processed to provide the specific feature you have requested. We do not sell your content. Content transmitted to our AI service providers is retained and processed in accordance with those providers’ terms and data-governance commitments, which may permit them to retain content for limited periods (for example, to provide the service, ensure quality, or for safety and abuse monitoring). We do not direct providers to use your content to train their general-purpose models, but their terms govern their actual practices. We do not control, and this policy does not warrant, every aspect of an AI service provider’s internal processing, and we do not make, and you should not rely on, any absolute representation that submitted content is never retained or never used to improve a provider’s models.

AI features have inherent limitations. AI-generated output may be incomplete or inaccurate and should not be relied upon as professional, legal, safety, engineering, or financial advice. You are responsible for reviewing and verifying AI-generated output before relying on it.

Some content you provide to AI features may originate from third parties (for example, documents or images authored by others). You are responsible for ensuring you have the necessary rights and permissions to submit such content for processing.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your content and account information are stored using third-party cloud hosting and infrastructure providers on our behalf. We rely on industry-standard safeguards — including encryption in transit, access controls, authenticated access to backend services, and best-effort application attestation for AI service requests — to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

Diagnostics may include in-app content. To diagnose and fix problems, the Service collects diagnostic, error, and crash reports and sends them to our crash-reporting providers. These reports are associated with your account identifier so we can investigate issues affecting your account. We apply automated measures designed to remove certain identifiers (such as email addresses, authentication tokens, and similar credentials and internal record paths) from these reports, and we limit the structured fields attached to error records. This automated redaction targets specific identifier patterns; it does not remove free-form text or content you have entered or spoken, so any content included in a diagnostic message is retained as-is after that pattern-based redaction.

During voice interactions, transcribed portions of your spoken input and Agent K’s responses, and the parameters of actions you trigger by voice (such as project or task names you dictate), are written to diagnostic breadcrumb logs that are sent to our crash-reporting providers, whether or not an error occurs. Our automated redaction removes structured identifiers (such as email addresses and tokens) but does not remove free-form spoken content. Please do not include sensitive information in voice interactions that you would not want captured in diagnostic logs.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your sign-in credentials and for the security of the devices you use to access the Service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, and thereafter as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and maintain the security and integrity of the Service.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account at any time from within the application, in the settings area. Account deletion is a confirmed, irreversible action that removes your account and is intended to delete your projects and associated content. When you delete your account:

  • Your authentication identity (your sign-in credential) is permanently removed, and where you signed in using a third-party method that supports it (such as Apple), we additionally request revocation of the associated sign-in token.
  • Your projects, content, and associated records are immediately removed from active use in the Service and no longer appear or are accessible to you through the application. Note that this in-app deletion marks your records as deleted and hides them from the application; it does not by itself erase the underlying records, any uploaded files, locally cached copies on your device, or diagnostic records associated with your account (see “Residual and deferred deletion” below).
  • Our account-deletion process does not automatically remove diagnostic, error, and analytics records, which may be associated with your account identifier and are retained and deleted in accordance with the practices described in Section 5 and our providers’ retention practices.

Residual and deferred deletion. For technical and operational reasons, certain copies of data are not erased the instant you delete your account. Records that have been removed from active use, along with associated stored files, are removed from active use immediately and are intended to be permanently deleted thereafter. We are continuing to build out the automated processes that permanently erase deleted records and their associated stored files; until those processes complete a given deletion, soft-deleted records and uploaded files may persist in our systems. We do not currently guarantee a specific timeframe for permanent erasure. Residual copies may also persist temporarily in backups, logs, and diagnostic records until those are overwritten, expire, or are purged in the ordinary course. Diagnostic, error, and analytics records, and information held by our service providers, are subject to those systems’ and providers’ own retention practices and are not removed by the in-app account-deletion action. We retain and use de-identified or aggregated information that no longer identifies you without limitation, and we may retain information where required or permitted by applicable law.
