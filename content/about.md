Kentro AI Labs is a boutique AI studio that builds **custom AI for construction and trades businesses** — trained on your documents, your knowledge, and your way of working, so it fits your business instead of forcing your business to fit it.

## What we build

Custom AI that takes the back-office work off your team — trained on your jobs, not the internet. We build three kinds: [document processing](/solutions/document-processing/) that reads and checks your invoices, [AI assistants](/solutions/ai-assistants/) that give instant sourced answers from your own records, and [AI agents](/solutions/ai-agents/) that run the routine chase-and-collect work for you. A person always stays in control of anything that matters.

## Not the generic AI everyone already has

ChatGPT and Copilot are the same for you as for your competitor down the street; they don't know your business, and no one quite trusts them with the real work. We build AI that works alongside your team — it knows your business, does your actual tasks, and you own it.

- **Trained on you, not the internet.** Generic AI was trained on the web, not on your invoices, your policies, your customers, or your process. Ours is.
- **Works with your team.** An assistant or a teammate is something you collaborate with and stay in control of — not a black box that replaces people.
- **Boutique is the advantage.** Being small is exactly why we can tailor each build to one business. A big firm sells you a template; a SaaS product sells everyone the same thing. We don't.
- **You own it.** It's built around your business, and it's yours to keep.

## What we are not

- Not a SaaS product you sign up for — a custom build for your business.
- Not generic AI — not ChatGPT, Copilot, or a one-size-fits-all bot.
- Not a big-consultancy project — boutique, focused, fast, and fixed in scope.

## We build and run AI in construction

We don't just talk about AI in construction — we ship it. [Agent K](/products/agent-k/) is our own product: a voice-and-text app crews use on the job site to log photos and track issues, hands-free. It runs in real conditions, on real projects. The same standard goes into everything we build for you.

## How we work

A clear path, a fixed price, no surprises. A free discovery call to learn your business and tell you honestly where AI would help. A fixed-scope, fixed-price build agreed in writing before we start. Then we run it with you and improve it as your business changes.

**Honest promise:** if AI isn't worth it for your business yet, we'll tell you on the first call.
