### Photo Documentation

Snap a photo on site and Agent K logs it, tags it to the right project, and flags anything that needs attention.

### Conversational Tasks

Tell Agent K what needs doing in plain language. It creates tasks, assigns priorities, and follows up so nothing falls through the cracks.

Start each day with a summary of what matters — overdue items, upcoming milestones, and what your team accomplished yesterday.

### Voice Capture

Talk through your day on the drive home. Agent K transcribes, extracts action items, and updates your projects automatically.
