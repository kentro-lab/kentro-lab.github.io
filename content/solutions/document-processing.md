## The problem

**Every sub and supplier sends invoices a different way.**

One emails a PDF. One texts a photo of a paper slip. One hands your foreman a crumpled carbon copy. Your office manager retypes each one into QuickBooks, matches it to the right purchase order and the right job, and chases down the ones that look wrong. It eats hours every week — and things still slip through.

Overbillings get caught late, or not at all. A wrong quantity, a rate that doesn't match the PO, a duplicate invoice for work already billed — by the time anyone notices, the check has cleared. The work is tedious, it's error-prone, and it's exactly the kind of work AI is good at.

## How it works

**Four stages, no data entry.** The system reads each invoice however it arrives, checks it against what you already agreed to, and posts the clean ones straight into your accounting — handing your admin only the ones that don't add up.

1. **It arrives.** An invoice lands however it comes — an emailed PDF, a photo, a scan, or a paper slip your office snaps a picture of.
2. **It reads and extracts.** The AI pulls out the vendor, the amount, and the line items — and notes how confident it is in each field.
3. **It checks.** It matches the invoice against your purchase order and your job budget — quantities, rates, and totals, line by line.
4. **It posts, or it flags.** Clean invoices post straight into QuickBooks. Anything that doesn't match goes to your admin to approve before a cent is paid.
