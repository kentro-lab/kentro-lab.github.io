## The problem

**Before you can pay a sub, you have to go collect the paperwork.**

Before you can pay a subcontractor or bill a client, you need signed lien waivers and current insurance certificates from everyone on the job. Tracking who's missing what, chasing them down, checking the paperwork, and filing it is a whole day of nagging — every single pay cycle.

Miss one and it holds up a payment or puts you at risk. It's the part of the job nobody wants to do, and it comes back around every time you pay.

## How it works

**An assistant answers. An agent acts.**

An AI agent doesn't just tell you who's missing a document — it runs the whole loop. It checks who's missing a signed lien waiver or a current insurance cert, requests it from each sub, follows up until they respond, validates what comes back, files it, updates your system, and hands you only the exceptions.

- **An assistant answers — it tells you what it knows.** Ask "who's missing a waiver?" and it gives you the list. Useful — but you still have to send the requests, follow up, check the replies, and file everything yourself.
- **An agent acts — it does the work for you.** It sends the requests, follows up until each sub responds, validates what comes back, files what's clean, updates your system — and flags only the exceptions. Think of it as an AI teammate, not a chatbot.

## Watch the loop

**The part a person hates doing — it just keeps doing.**

Most of the effort in this job isn't the first request. It's the following up: the second email, the text three days later, the call the week after. That persistence is the whole point — the agent keeps chasing each sub until the document is in.

1. **It checks who's missing.** A payment run triggers it. It looks across your subs and flags anyone without a signed waiver or a current cert.
2. **It requests from each sub.** It sends each one exactly what it needs — the right document, for the right job.
3. **It follows up until done.** No reply, or the document isn't valid? It loops back and asks again — and again — until the paperwork comes in. This is the part you never have to touch.
4. **It files what's clean, flags the rest.** Valid documents get validated, filed, and logged in your system. Only real exceptions come to you.
