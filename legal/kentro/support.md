# Support

**Kentro AI Labs Inc.**

Need a hand with Kentro? We're happy to help.

## Contact us

Email **support@kentrolabs.ai** — we aim to reply within 2 business days. Please
include the email address on your account and, where it helps, a short
description or a screenshot of the issue.

## Common questions

**How do I delete my account and my data?**
In the app, open **Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account**. This removes your
account and is intended to delete your projects and content. See our
[Privacy Policy](https://kentrolabs.ai/kentro/privacy) for details on retention
and deletion.

**How is my data handled?**
Everything is covered in our [Privacy Policy](https://kentrolabs.ai/kentro/privacy)
— what we collect, how AI features process your content, and your choices.

**I found a bug, or I have feedback.**
Email **support@kentrolabs.ai** with what happened and, if you can, a screenshot.
Details help us fix things faster.
