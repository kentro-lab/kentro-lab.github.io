# Step 1 — Positioning and the 3 Solutions

Status: **draft for Ali's review.** This is the foundation every later step builds on.

## Positioning statement

**Kentro AI Labs is a boutique AI studio that builds custom AI for small businesses** — trained on your documents, your knowledge, and your way of working, so it fits your business instead of forcing your business to fit it.

Not the generic AI everyone already has. ChatGPT and Copilot are the same for you as for your competitor down the street; they don't know your business and no one quite trusts them with the real work. We build AI that **works alongside your team** — it knows your business, does your actual tasks, and you own it.

### In the classic form (useful for downstream copy)

> For **small-business owners** who know AI could help but don't want a generic tool or a big-consultancy project, **Kentro AI Labs** is a **boutique AI studio** that **builds custom AI trained on your own business and working alongside your team** — unlike ChatGPT, Copilot, or off-the-shelf software, because **it is built around how your business actually runs, not the other way around.**

---

## Who it's for

- **Primary:** owners and operators of **small businesses** — non-technical. They run the business; they are not engineers and don't want to become one.
- **Their mindset:** "I keep hearing AI could save me time, but the generic tools feel risky or useless for my actual work, and I don't have an IT department to build something real."
- **What they want to hear:** that this will fit *their* business, work *with* their people, be trustworthy on real tasks, and not turn into an endless expensive project.
- **Not our primary audience:** engineers, big enterprises, or heavily regulated industries (healthcare, finance, legal, accounting) — we avoid those for now to sidestep the SOC2/HIPAA certification burden. (Niche selection is Step 2.)

---

## The differentiator (the spine of everything)

**Custom AI, trained on your business, that works alongside your team — not generic ChatGPT or Copilot.**

Why it matters and why it's credible for a boutique:
- **Generic AI doesn't know your business.** It was trained on the internet, not on your invoices, your policies, your customers, or your process. Ours is.
- **"Works with you" builds trust.** An assistant or a teammate is something you collaborate with and stay in control of — not a black box that replaces people. This is the emotional reason a cautious owner says yes.
- **Boutique is the advantage, not the apology.** Being small is exactly why we can tailor each build to one business. A big firm sells you a template; a SaaS product sells everyone the same thing. We don't.
- **You own it.** It's built around your business and it's yours to keep.

## The three solutions — *Read → Answer → Do*

One intuitive spine a non-technical owner can hold: AI that **understands what comes in**, **answers what people ask**, and **does the work that follows**. Each is built around *that* business — nothing off-the-shelf.

### 1. Document processing
**Tagline:** *Paperwork, handled.*
Reads the invoices, receipts, forms, orders, and emails that pile up, and turns them into clean, correct data inside the tools you already use — with a person checking anything unusual.
**Custom angle:** built around your document types, your rules, and your systems.
**Why it leads:** the most concrete, most relatable entry point for a small business, and an area few boutique competitors own well.

### 2. AI assistants
**Tagline:** *An assistant that actually knows your business.*
A helper trained on your own business — your documents, policies, products, and past conversations — that answers questions instantly, for your customers or your team. Unlike ChatGPT, its answers come from *your* business, not the whole internet.
**Custom angle:** grounded in your knowledge; speaks in your voice; works with your people.

### 3. AI agents
**Tagline:** *An AI teammate that does the routine work.*
Handles routine, multi-step jobs from start to finish — intake, checks, updates, follow-ups — connected to your existing tools, with a person in charge of the exceptions. An assistant *answers*; an agent *acts*.
**Custom angle:** built around your process; a teammate that takes the repetitive work off your plate while you stay in control.

**How they relate:** they stack. A business can start with paperwork (1), add an assistant that answers from that same information (2), and grow into agents that run whole processes (3). Start small, expand as trust grows.

## Voice and tone principles

- **Plain language, warm, confident.** Talk about the owner's work and time, not the technology. No jargon in the main message ("agentic," "RAG," "LLM" never appear in the owner-facing copy).
- **Trust before capability.** Lead with "works with you, stays in your control, built for you," then show what it does.
- **Borrow polish, not voice.** From the reference sites (Freshworks, ServiceNow, Temporal, CrewAI, Across) we take the modern visual craft and clear workflow diagrams — not their developer-facing language.
- **Show, don't claim.** Value is demonstrated with simple diagrams and short before/after stories (built in Step 3), not adjectives.
- **Firm stays anonymous** — "we / our team," no founder identity.
