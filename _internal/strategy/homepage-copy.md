## The one decision baked in here: lead explicitly with construction

The homepage names **construction and trades** directly. The whole reason to niche is so a construction owner lands and instantly feels "this is for *me*" — the opposite of the generic feeling we're fighting. Agent K as proof only works inside construction. Home/field services is served as the adjacent, but the homepage leads with construction and trades so the message stays sharp.

*(If you'd rather keep the homepage broader — "small businesses" with construction as the lead example — say so; it's a copy tweak, not a rebuild. My strong recommendation is to lead with construction.)*

## Section flow (with draft copy)

Clean, modern, Freshworks-style polish — but the content (construction, real workflows, block diagrams, Agent K) is what makes it specific and un-templated. The **block-diagram-per-solution is the signature device.**

### 1. Nav
Wordmark "Kentro AI Labs" · links: What we build · How it works · Agent K · **Book a call** (button).

### 2. Hero
Headline + subhead + support line + primary/secondary CTA (per above). Visual: a simple hero diagram — *your business → custom AI → your team* — or a clean construction-flavored illustration. Kept light; the real diagrams come in section 4.

### 3. The reality (empathy — short, construction-specific)
> **You didn't start a construction business to do data entry.**
> But between the invoices, the RFIs, the lien waivers, and the same five questions your office answers every day, the admin work took over. It's the part nobody wants — and it's exactly what AI is good at.

### 4. What we build — the three solutions (the signature section)
> **Three kinds of custom AI, built around how you work.**
> Not an app you sign up for. We build each one around your business — your jobs, your subs, your systems — and connect it to the tools you already run.

Three blocks, each: **name + tagline → block diagram → one or two lines of value → the "a person stays in control" note → "Learn more" link** to its solution page.

- **Document processing — *Paperwork, handled.*** [invoice → extract → match to PO → post / flag] · "Invoices read, checked against your POs, and posted to QuickBooks — with your admin approving only the exceptions."
- **AI assistants — *An assistant that actually knows your business.*** [your project data → assistant → sourced answer] · "Instant, sourced answers to 'what's the spec' and 'what's the status' — from your jobs, not the internet."
- **AI agents — *An AI teammate that does the routine work.*** [trigger → chase-and-collect loop → file / flag] · "Chases subs for lien waivers and insurance certs, collects and files them, and hands you only the exceptions."

### 5. Why it's different
> **Not ChatGPT. Not another app. Yours.**
Four short points (no jargon):
- **Trained on your business, not the internet.** It knows your jobs, your subs, your prices.
- **Works with your team.** A person stays in control of anything that matters.
- **Built around your tools.** It fits into QuickBooks, your PM software, your email — no new system to learn.
- **Yours to keep.** You own what we build.

### 6. Proof — Agent K
> **We don't just talk about AI in construction. We ship it.**
> Agent K is our own product — a voice-and-text app crews use on the job site to log photos and track issues, hands-free. It's proof we build AI that holds up in real conditions, not demos.
Link: **See Agent K →** (`/products/agent-k`).

### 7. How we work
> **A clear path, a fixed price, no surprises.**
1. **Discovery call** — free. We learn your business and tell you honestly where AI would help, and where it wouldn't.
2. **Build** — a fixed scope and a fixed price, agreed in writing before we start. You own everything we build.
3. **Run it with you** — we keep it working and improve it as your business changes.
Honest line: *If AI isn't worth it for your business yet, we'll tell you on the first call.*

### 8. Final CTA
> **See where AI could save your team the most time.**
> Book a free discovery call. No jargon, no obligation — just an honest read on what's worth building.
**Book a discovery call** · *Prefer email? hello@kentrolabs.ai*
Secondary teaser: *Not sure where to start? Take the 2-minute readiness check →* (links to the readiness page).

### 9. Footer
© 2026 Kentro AI Labs Inc · Agent K · Privacy · Terms · Support · hello@kentrolabs.ai. Anonymous — no founder identity.

## CTA strategy

- **One primary action everywhere: book a discovery call** (`mailto:hello@kentrolabs.ai?subject=Discovery%20call` for now; swap to a booking link later). Repeated at top, mid, and bottom.
- **Secondary, lower-commitment:** "See what we build" (scrolls to solutions) and the "2-minute readiness check" teaser (routes the unsure to the self-guide instead of losing them).
- **Honest disqualification** next to the final CTA — signals confidence, lowers the risk of booking.
