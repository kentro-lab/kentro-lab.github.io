## Selection criteria

1. **Market size** — enough small businesses to sustain a pipeline.
2. **Pain-point fit** — how well Document processing / AI assistants / AI agents map to their real, felt problems.
3. **Regulatory burden** — low is good.
4. **Ability + willingness to pay** — margins and budget for a custom build.
5. **Reachability** — non-technical owners a boutique can actually reach and win.
6. **Whitespace** — not already saturated with AI vendors.
7. **Stack depth** — can they grow across all three solutions (expansion path)?

## What the scoring says

- **Restaurants and retail fall away** despite huge markets — restaurants on willingness to pay (notoriously thin margins), retail on whitespace (saturated with e-commerce AI). Both are traps: big and reachable, but hard to win or hard to bill.
- **Real estate is good but not best** — strong solution fit, but more regulated-adjacent (transactions touch money and legal docs) and the most crowded with existing AI tools. Property management is the stronger sub-slice.
- **Logistics and manufacturing/distribution are excellent document-processing beachheads** — heavy paperwork, clear ROI, real B2B budgets, lots of whitespace — but they lean on solutions 1 and 3, with a weaker "assistant" story, and are harder to reach.
- **Construction and home/field services score highest** and, importantly, **share an owner profile**: a non-technical operator running the business from a truck or a job site, buried in back-office paperwork and customer calls. The messaging, the solutions, and the sales motion transfer between them — so choosing one doesn't waste work on the other.

But in your earlier message you named construction (alongside accountants and realtors) in a sentence I read two ways: as niches you're *considering*, or as niches to *avoid*. Accountants are clearly out (regulation). I need to know where construction and realtors sit for you before I finalize — because it swings the recommendation.

Two considerations on construction specifically:

- **Possibly against it:** if you want the consulting arm kept distinct from the Agent K product, or you personally don't want to specialize there.

## Recommendation (pending your call on construction)

- **If construction is in:** make **construction and the trades** the primary niche, anchored by Agent K as proof, with **home / field services** as the natural adjacent (same owner, same pains, shared messaging). This is the sharpest, best-supported focus available.

Either path is focused, non-regulated, large enough, and fits all three solutions. The only fork is construction in or out.
