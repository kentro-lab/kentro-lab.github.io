# Kentro Labs Redesign — Benchmark Dossier

Final benchmark set for the redesign of **kentrolabs.ai** (a small AI-engineering consultancy for SMBs). Each firm was chosen because it teaches something concrete about design or content strategy, is a real operation (not a shell), and is broadly comparable to Kentro. The set is deliberately spread across positioning styles: editorial/design-led, engineering/production-led, vertical-specialist, enablement/training-led, and productized-offer/diagnostic-led.

Sources: visual reports (Chrome walk-throughs, `visual_quality` 1-5) + fetch-based validation (`credibility` 1-5, `site_quality` 1-5). Screenshots in `research/screenshots/`.

## 1. Hexaa — https://thehexaa.ai
**Screenshot:** `research/screenshots/thehexaa-ai.png`
**Scale/credibility:** Credibility 4; scale unclear but real ("47+ engagements", "12M+ docs"). Visual quality 5 — the strongest craft in the batch.
**What they sell:** Custom document-automation / IDP pipelines (classification, extraction, validation, ERP/TMS/AP integration), client owns the code; fractional automation-team retainer. Niches: logistics, construction, insurance (P&C), AP, banking, healthcare.

- Interactive ROI/cost calculator with a volume slider that converts document volume into wasted FTEs ("1,000 hours/month, 6.3 FTEs") — turns an abstract pain into a number the buyer feels.
- "Anatomy of the workaround" before/after diagram color-codes manual vs system steps; engagement stages annotated with real durations (1 week / 4-6 weeks). Specificity is the trust device.

## Cross-cutting observations — what separates the strong sites from the weak

**1. A productized, self-serve entry offer beats a "Contact us" button.** The strongest sites all convert a felt problem into a named, low-risk first step: FirmAdapt's 8-lens domain scan, InitializeAI's Gap Score scorecard, Grace & Cronos's 30-day Assessment, aiChemist's $1,500 roadmap, Hexaa's ROI calculator, HelloAgentic's Blueprint. Weak sites (4sight, Execucom's thin backing, the dropped generalist shops) stop at "Learn More / Contact Us."

**2. Show the work; don't assert it.** Live product mocks (Hexaa's invoice card, InitializeAI's scorecard), animated real pipelines (Enso's terminal, WhiteBox's forecasting chart, HelloAgentic's cost ticker) all *demonstrate* capability. This is far more convincing than adjective-heavy copy, and it's what the AI-slop sites (4sight's buzzword wheel, stock robot imagery) conspicuously lack.

**3. Trust is engineered differently depending on whether you have logos.** Firms *with* recognizable clients lead with them (WhiteBox's DoD/Vodafone wall, GoGloby's Zillow/Hasbro, Frontier Foundry's DoE/State). Firms *without* substitute deliberately: quantified-but-anonymized testimonials (Beige Bananas), borrowed industry proof (GreenData's "Entourage"), radical honesty (Sense7ai), named-founder credibility + IP-ownership guarantees (aiChemist, HelloAgentic), or risk-reversal guarantees (GoGloby's $3M liability + 120-day guarantee). Kentro, without a marquee logo wall, should copy the second playbook.

**4. Named, proprietary IP frames a small firm as a real practice.** Leverage Index (Grace & Cronos), ARC (Human Pilots), AIT Method™ (Execucom), "AI Execution Gap" (InitializeAI), "three-part sovereignty test" (Frontier Foundry), Seven Beige Principles. A named framework + a diagram turns a service into a methodology.

**5. A single, committed visual point of view wins.** The vq5/vq4 sites each own one device: serif-italic-in-sans (Hexaa, Enso), warm serif-on-peach (Evren), section markers §01 (Enso), a literal equation graphic (Beige Bananas), thin-red-line diagrams (Frontier Foundry). Restraint — one accent color, generous whitespace — reads as premium (HelloAgentic, WhiteBox). The weak sites default to navy-gradient-plus-stock-neural-mesh (Fintechy) or busy WordPress card stacks (NeoLumin, Opinosis) with no authored device.

**6. Reframe the buyer's problem before selling the solution.** The best copy diagnoses first: "A demo is not a system" (HelloAgentic), "they have a prioritization and execution problem" (Grace & Cronos), "execution readiness is missing, not ideas" (InitializeAI), "74% of AI projects fail — it's the diagnosis, not the tech" (aiChemist), "AI Readiness is a score, not a feeling" (Human Pilots). A sharp contrarian thesis outperforms a feature list.

**7. AEO/LLM-awareness is an emerging trust signal.** Evren's "FAQ for buyers and AI assistants," Human Pilots' published llms.txt + ethics policy, Anveshi's "be the firm AI recommends" GEO angle. Being legible to AI search is becoming table stakes for AI consultancies specifically.

**8. Contrast/legibility is non-negotiable — a great concept can be undone by execution.** Anveshi's expedition concept is strong but its teal-on-busy-photo hero is genuinely hard to read; several dropped sites duplicate hero blocks at page bottom (build errors that read as sloppiness). Concept earns attention; execution keeps trust.
