# Five homepage candidates — design & content directions

Derived from the 20-firm benchmark dossier (`research/dossier.md`) and its cross-cutting observations. Each candidate takes a DIFFERENT position on: primary audience emphasis, conversion mechanism, trust playbook, and aesthetic. All five must obey the SHARED RULES at the bottom.

Kentro's real capabilities (the honest raw material for every candidate): intelligent document processing (invoice/receipt extraction, communication mining) · AI chatbots & agentic workflows grounded in client data and systems · AI enablement/training for teams · substantiated by deep software engineering — full-stack, backend, data-intensive systems, cloud (GCP & AWS), ML platforms, 15+ years of team experience. Kentro also ships its own product (Agent K, AI project management for construction) — footer link only.

## Candidate 1 — "Blueprint" · productized-offer clarity
**File:** `candidates/c1-blueprint.html`
**Bet:** the buyer books when the engagement has a concrete, named shape. (Benchmarks: HelloAgentic's fixed-shape offers + positioning matrix; Hexaa's restraint; aiChemist's price-anchored entry.)

- **Audience emphasis:** SMB owner who has been burned or fears being burned; secondary technical reader.
- **Conversion mechanism:** three named, fixed-shape engagements — **Assess** (fixed-fee, 2 weeks, written findings + build/no-build recommendation), **Build** (fixed scope, fixed price agreed in writing), **Operate** (monthly retainer, monitoring + improvement). No dollar figures; shape is the promise. Each card states exactly what the client receives.
- **Trust playbook (no logos):** IP ownership ("you own the code, the prompts, the data"), no-lock-in pledge, positioning matrix (freelancer / Kentro / big consultancy across 5 rows: who does the work, scope shape, production ownership, cost structure, what happens after launch), honest-disqualification line near CTA.
- **Aesthetic:** near-white paper `#FBFBFA`, ink `#111418`, ONE hot accent `#E8490F` used surgically (CTAs, key words, one rule); Inter body + IBM Plex Mono labels; display via Inter Tight (tight, large, confident). Hero pairs the thesis with an illustrative product-style card: an "agent run" panel (steps, checks, cost-per-task line) clearly captioned as illustrative. Whitespace is the luxury; zero decoration elsewhere.
- **Hero thesis:** "A demo is easy. A system is the work." (diagnose-first copy; complete professional sentences.)

## Candidate 2 — "Field Notes" · dark editorial studio
**File:** `candidates/c2-fieldnotes.html`
**Bet:** the buyer trusts a studio with a strong authored point of view and visible engineering culture. (Benchmarks: Enso Labs' §-marker editorial system + terminal proof; WhiteBox's restrained dark craft.)

- **Audience emphasis:** technical founders/teams first; sophisticated owners second.
- **Conversion mechanism:** a single strong CTA ("Book a discovery call") plus an honest availability line ("We take a small number of engagements at a time"). Quality of thinking is the funnel.
- **Trust playbook:** proof-of-craft — an animated terminal in the hero that types out an agent pipeline run (ingest → plan → execute tools → eval gate → deploy; labeled "simulation for illustration"); "notes from production" — 3 anonymized capability notes written like engineering field notes ("Our team has shipped…", honestly framed as prior team experience, no client names, no invented metrics); a method section (evals before launch, observability, rollback paths).
- **Aesthetic:** near-black `#0C0E12`, warm off-white text, mono microtype, section markers `§ 01 — POSITIONING` etc.; display = Fraunces italic serif at oversized sizes mixed with a grotesque; single signal color amber `#F5A524`. Sparse, cinematic spacing. Motion: the terminal type-out only; reduced-motion renders final state.
- **Hero thesis:** "Strategy to ship." with a subline that names the gap between prototypes and production systems.

## Candidate 3 — "Diagnostic" · interactive readiness check
**File:** `candidates/c3-diagnostic.html`
**Bet:** a self-serve diagnostic converts better than any copy — give value before asking for the call. (Benchmarks: FirmAdapt's 8-lens scan + HUD aesthetic; InitializeAI's scorecard hero; Grace & Cronos's routing.)

- **Audience emphasis:** both audiences; the check routes each to the right conversation.
- **Conversion mechanism:** hero = **"AI Production-Readiness Check"** — 8 plain-language yes/no questions (client-side JS only, no tracking, instant result). Result: score /8, a named tier (e.g. "Exploring / Piloting / Production-ready"), the two lenses to fix first, and a prefilled mailto CTA that includes their tier in the subject. Named framework: "Production Readiness Index — eight lenses" (data, workflow fit, integration, evaluation, oversight, cost model, security, team adoption). Each lens gets a one-sentence explanation — the framework itself demonstrates expertise.
- **Trust playbook:** the diagnostic IS the trust device (give value first); honest scoring notes ("a self-assessment, not an audit — the Assess engagement is the real version"); services presented as "where each result leads."
- **Aesthetic:** deep blue-teal `#0B1D2A` HUD with restrained glow, serif display (Source Serif 4) contrasting mono UI chips (`60 SEC · FREE · NO EMAIL`), per-lens small accent colors used in the module grid only; light section alternation for services. Must stay legible and professional — HUD flavor, not sci-fi noise.
- **Hero thesis:** "Find out if your business is ready for AI in production — in two minutes."

## Candidate 4 — "Warm Counsel" · humanist SMB-owner-first
**File:** `candidates/c4-warmcounsel.html`
**Bet:** SMB owners buy from the firm that meets them where they are, in their language, without tech intimidation. (Benchmarks: Cocoon's situation self-selector; Evren's warm palette; Beige Bananas' personality; Anveshi's humanism minus its contrast mistakes.)

- **Audience emphasis:** non-technical SMB owner, unapologetically.
- **Conversion mechanism:** four **buyer-situation cards** as the primary interaction — "We're drowning in paperwork" / "We know AI could help, but not where to start" / "We tried a pilot and it stalled" / "We want our team actually using AI well." Each expands (details/summary or JS) to: what's usually going on → what we'd do → which engagement it leads to → CTA.
- **Trust playbook:** plain-spoken honesty ("Sometimes the answer is that AI isn't worth it for you yet — we'll say so on the first call"), a "how an engagement actually goes" week-by-week timeline, augment-not-replace stance ("your people get their hours back"), IP ownership in plain words.
- **Aesthetic:** cream `#FAF4EC` → soft peach section bands, deep navy ink `#1C2340`, coral accent `#DE5935`; Fraunces display + Instrument Sans body; pill buttons, soft-radius cards, subtle paper-grid texture; small bespoke SVG spot illustrations (geometric, hand-drawn feel — documents, gears, people shapes) — NO stock imagery, NO robots. High contrast everywhere (learn from Anveshi's failure).
- **Hero thesis:** "AI that quietly does the boring work, so your people can do the real work."

## Candidate 5 — "Ledger" · Swiss precision + radical honesty
**File:** `candidates/c5-ledger.html`
**Bet:** in a category full of inflated claims, verifiable honesty is the sharpest differentiator. (Benchmarks: Sense7ai's earned-not-claimed copy; Elucidate's SLA-tied specifics; Execucom's objection-handling; GoGloby's risk-reversal shape.)

- **Audience emphasis:** the diligent buyer — owner or tech lead — who reads everything before booking.
- **Conversion mechanism:** "Schedule a scoping call" as a productized step with a stated response SLA ("we reply within one business day"); an engagement **spec-sheet table** (stage / duration / what you receive / how it's priced) written like a term sheet.
- **Trust playbook:** the signature is an honesty ledger — two columns: **"What we'll claim"** (senior team, 15+ years across distributed systems and ML platforms; we build and run our own production AI product; you own everything we build; fixed prices in writing) vs **"What we won't claim (yet)"** (no client logos to show yet — the firm is new; no certifications we haven't earned; no guaranteed outcomes before seeing your data). Plus an objection-handling FAQ quoting realistic buyer worries verbatim ("How do I know this won't be another stalled pilot?"), each answered concretely.
- **Aesthetic:** Swiss grid discipline — cool paper `#F4F5F4`, near-black ink, deep green accent `#175A3C`; Archivo (tight, medium weights) + IBM Plex Mono for tabular data; hairline rules used structurally (tables, ledger), right-aligned numerals, zero illustration; typography and alignment ARE the design. Confident restraint.
- **Hero thesis:** "We'd rather earn your trust than claim it."

## SHARED RULES (every candidate — hard requirements)

1. **Honesty:** anonymous firm (no founder names/photos; "we/our team"); NO invented client logos, testimonials, case studies, or metrics; anonymized team experience must be framed as such; illustrative UI/terminal mocks labeled illustrative; any cited statistic must be real and attributed (or omitted).
2. **Voice:** professional and measured — complete sentences, technical specificity, zero hype words, no cheeky fragments, sentence case. (Owner feedback from v1: copy must not read simplistic or gimmicky.)
3. **Brand:** "Kentro AI Labs" wordmark; company line "Kentro AI Labs Inc"; domain kentrolabs.ai.
4. **CTA:** every candidate's primary CTA → `mailto:hello@kentrolabs.ai?subject=Discovery%20call` (C3 may append tier info to the subject; C5 may use `?subject=Scoping%20call`).
5. **Tech:** ONE self-contained HTML file (inline `<style>`, inline JS); only external resource = Google Fonts (preconnect + single stylesheet link); no build step; works from `file://` and any static host.
6. **Head:** proper `<title>`, meta description, canonical `https://kentrolabs.ai/`, OG tags, `lang="en"`, viewport, inline SVG data-URI favicon consistent with the candidate's palette.
7. **Quality floor:** responsive to 360 px with no horizontal scroll; visible `:focus-visible` styles; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (all motion optional); WCAG AA contrast; semantic headings; nav via in-page anchors only (no dead links).
8. **Footer:** © 2026 Kentro AI Labs Inc · Agent K → `/products/agent-k` · Privacy → `/kentro/privacy` · Terms → `/kentro/terms` · Support → `/kentro/support` · `hello@kentrolabs.ai`.
9. **Sections every candidate must cover somewhere, in its own way:** what we build (the three service areas, concretely, with named example builds) · engineering depth line · how an engagement works · honest-disqualification note · final CTA. Beyond that, structure serves the candidate's bet.
