Where you are right now

Most owners do not arrive with a technical brief. They arrive with a feeling that something should be easier. Find the one below that sounds like your week, and we will tell you plainly what is usually going on and what we would do about it.

“We're drowning in paperwork.”
What's usually going on

Invoices, receipts, order forms, and email threads arrive in a dozen formats, and a capable person is spending hours re-typing them into your accounting or ERP system. The work is not hard, but it is relentless, error-prone at the end of a long day, and it is quietly capping how much your team can take on.

What we'd do

We build an intelligent document pipeline that reads each document, pulls out the fields you actually use, checks them against your existing records, and flags only the ones a person needs to look at. It writes the results straight into the systems you already run. The goal is not a clever demo; it is fewer keystrokes every single morning.

Where this leads: a short, fixed-fee assessment to measure your real document volume and formats, then a build scoped to the highest-volume documents first.

“We tried a pilot and it stalled.”
What's usually going on

A demo worked in a meeting, everyone was excited, and then it never became part of the day. Almost always the gap is between a prototype and a system: no connection to your live data, no handling of the messy cases, no way to tell when it is wrong, and no owner once the initial enthusiasm faded. A demo is easy; a system that runs every day is the real work.

What we'd do

We start by finding out exactly where it broke down — the data, the integration, the accuracy, or the adoption. Then we rebuild the parts that matter with the plumbing a real system needs: grounding in your data, checks that catch mistakes before they reach a customer, monitoring so problems surface early, and a clear path to roll back when something changes.

Where this leads: a focused assessment of the stalled pilot, then either a scoped rebuild or an honest recommendation to retire it, depending on what we find.

What we build

Three kinds of work, and the systems behind them.

Everything we do falls into one of three areas. Each one is a real, running system, not a slide. Here is what they are and the kind of thing we would actually build for you.

Document processing

Turning the steady stream of paperwork into clean, checked data in the systems you already run, with a person in the loop only where judgment is genuinely needed.

  • Invoice and receipt extraction that reconciles against your ledger
  • Order and form intake wired straight into your ERP or accounting tool
  • Mining email and message threads for the facts buried in them

Chatbots and agents

Assistants and agentic workflows grounded in your own data and connected to your own systems, so the answers are about your business and the actions actually happen.

  • A support assistant answering from your documents and policies
  • An internal helper that looks up records and drafts the reply
  • An agent that carries a routine multi-step task end to end

Team enablement

Hands-on training that gets your people using AI tools well and safely, built around the tasks they do every day rather than a generic curriculum.

  • Working sessions using real tasks from your team's week
  • Plain guidelines on what is safe to share and what is not
  • A simple way to check output before anyone trusts it

How an engagement actually goes

A first engagement is short, and you own what comes out of it.

We keep the first step small and concrete so the risk stays small. Here is roughly how the early weeks tend to go. Timings vary with the work, but the shape holds.

01 Week one

We listen

We spend time with the people who do the work, watch where the hours and errors go, and get specific about the problem before proposing anything.

02 Week two

We recommend

You get a short written assessment: what is worth doing, in what order, roughly what it takes, and a clear build or do-not-build recommendation. It is yours to keep.

03 If we build

We build in the open

Scope and price are agreed in writing before we start. We build the highest-value piece first and show you working software early, not at the very end.

04 After launch

We make it stick

We hand over code, prompts, and data that you own, train the people who will use it, and can stay on to monitor and improve it if that is what you want.

Straight talk

We would rather tell you the truth than take the work.

Sometimes the answer is no.

Not every business needs AI yet, and some problems are cheaper to fix with a spreadsheet, a process change, or a plain piece of software. When that is the case, we will say so on the first call, before you have spent anything with us. We would rather be the firm you trust than the one that sold you something you did not need.

  • You own everything. The code, the prompts, and the data are yours. No lock-in, and nothing held hostage.
  • We augment, we do not replace. The point is to give your people their hours back, so they spend them on the work only people can do.
  • Fixed scope, agreed in writing. You know what you are getting and what it costs before we build a thing.
  • Plain language, always. If we cannot explain what we are building in words your whole team understands, we have not thought about it hard enough.
