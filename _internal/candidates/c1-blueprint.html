What we build

Three kinds of system, built to run in production.

We work in the places where documents pile up, questions repeat, and people spend hours on work a well-built system can do reliably. Every build is grounded in your own data and connected to the tools you already use.

01

Document processing

Pull structured, verified data out of the paperwork your business runs on, with confidence scoring and a human-review path for anything the system is unsure about.

Example builds
  • Invoice and receipt extraction into your accounting system
  • Contract and statement parsing with field-level confidence
  • Communication mining across email and support inboxes
02

Chatbots and agentic workflows

Assistants and multi-step agents that answer from your own knowledge and act inside your systems, with evaluation gates that decide what runs automatically and what a person checks first.

Example builds
  • Support assistant grounded in your documentation and tickets
  • Internal agent that drafts, looks up, and updates records
  • Retrieval over your knowledge base with cited sources
03

AI enablement for your team

Practical training and working patterns so your people use these tools well and safely — not a one-off workshop, but habits and guardrails that hold after we leave.

Example builds

  • Guardrails and usage policy your team can follow

How we engage

Three engagements, each with a fixed shape.

You always know what you are buying and what you will receive before we begin. The shape is the promise; the deliverables below are the specifics.

Assess · 2 weeks → Build · fixed scope → Operate · monthly
01 — start here

Assess

Fixed fee · two weeks

We look at one candidate workflow, your data, and your systems, then tell you plainly whether to build it — and if so, exactly what.

You receive
  • A written findings document, not a slide deck
  • A clear build-or-do-not-build recommendation
  • Scoped design and cost estimate for the Build, if warranted
  • The data and integration risks we found, named honestly
02 — the build

Build

Fixed scope · fixed price

We build the system defined in the Assess to a scope and price agreed in writing, and deploy it into your workflow rather than handing over a prototype.

You receive
  • A working system deployed in your environment
  • Evaluation tests and a human-review path built in
  • Source code, prompts, and configuration, handed to you
  • Documentation your team can maintain from
03 — keep it running

Operate

Monthly retainer

Models drift, data changes, and edge cases surface in the real world. We monitor the system, keep it accurate, and improve it as your needs move.

You receive
  • Monitoring of accuracy, cost, and failures in production
  • Regular tuning as your data and volumes shift
  • A named point of contact and a response commitment
  • No lock-in — you can take operations in-house at any time

How an engagement works

From first call to a system that runs.

Most engagements move through these four stages. The first two are the Assess; the third is the Build; the fourth is the optional Operate retainer.

Week 0

Discovery call

A short conversation about the work you want to remove and the systems it touches. We tell you whether an Assess makes sense.

Weeks 1–2

Assess

We examine one workflow, your data, and your tools, then deliver written findings and a build-or-do-not-build recommendation.

Build window

Build and deploy

We build to the agreed scope, add evaluation and review paths, deploy into your environment, and hand over the code and documentation.

Ongoing

Operate

If you want it, we monitor and improve the system month to month. If you would rather run it yourself, we make sure you can.

Before you write

Sometimes the honest answer is that AI is not worth it for your business yet, or that a simpler tool would do the job. If that is what we find, we will say so in the Assess — and you will have paid a fixed fee for a clear answer rather than a stalled project.
